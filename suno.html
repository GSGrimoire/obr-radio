Copy a Suno playlist into the Radio

Suno won't let other sites read its playlists, but a bookmark on suno.com can read the page it's on. This one copies every song on the page in one go.

Once: add the bookmark

Drag this button to your browser's bookmarks bar:

Copy for Radio

Can't see a bookmarks bar? Press Ctrl+Shift+B (Cmd+Shift+B on a Mac).

Each time

  1. Open the playlist on suno.com. Scroll down until every song you want is shown, because Suno only puts songs on the page as you reach them.
  2. Press Copy for Radio in your bookmarks bar. It tells you how many songs it copied.
  3. In Owlbear, open Radio, pick or make a playlist, paste into the box and press Save.

Other ways that work

  • A song's address: https://suno.com/song/…
  • A song's whole embed code, as Suno's Share → Embed gives it
  • Any text with song links in it; every link is taken

Short share links (suno.com/s/…) can't be used. Open one and copy the address it turns into. Songs must be public for the table to hear them.

The bookmark only reads the page and writes to your clipboard. It sends nothing anywhere and changes nothing on Suno.
